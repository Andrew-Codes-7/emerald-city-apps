Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version: We collect as little as we can. Right now, the only personal information this website collects is the email address you give us if you join the launch list. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we are

Emerald City Apps ("we," "us") makes small, free apps for everyday life. This policy covers the website at emeraldcityapps.com. Each of our apps is covered below, and we'll update this page as new apps launch.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you type it into the "Notify me" form.
  • Basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. Our hosting provider records this automatically when any website is visited, to deliver pages and keep the site secure.

We don't ask for your name, address, phone number, or payment details on this site.

How we use it

  • To email you when our apps launch, and occasionally with news about Emerald City Apps.
  • To keep the website running, secure, and free of spam.

Every email we send will include a way to unsubscribe. You can also ask us to remove you at any time (see "Your choices" below).

Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share it with the services that help us run the site, and only so they can do that job:

  • Netlify hosts this website and stores launch-list signups.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts on this page, so your browser requests them from Google's servers.
  • Our email provider sends launch emails, if you've signed up.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Quiz Night

Quiz Night doesn't need an account and doesn't send your answers or scores to us. Player names, settings, and best scores are saved only in your own browser, and you can clear them by clearing your browser's site data. To show pictures and maps, the game loads flags, photos, and satellite images from public sources (jsDelivr, Wikimedia Commons, and EOX), so those services receive basic technical information like your IP address when the images load.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If we add website analytics in the future, we'll choose a privacy-friendly option and update this page first.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, or until we no longer need it for the launch list.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you at any time. Email hello@emeraldcityapps.com and we'll take care of it.

Children

The launch list is meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their email address, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

This site is served over a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we limit who can access the information we collect. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, but we work to protect yours.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we'll let people on the launch list know.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email hello@emeraldcityapps.com.
